Provenance of the files in this directory (spec 0007 REQ-WAK-010)
================================================================

*.s16, *.features.u16, frames.features.txt
  Generated by spikes/wakeword/make_golden.py. The audio is synthesised from a seeded random generator and formant
  filters (no recording of a person, no third-party audio). The feature files are what the reference frontend
  (pymicro-features 2.0.2, the TensorFlow Lite Micro audio frontend, Apache-2.0) produced for that audio. Licence:
  Apache-2.0, as the repository.
